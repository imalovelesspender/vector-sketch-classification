I finished all 14 screening runs on 345 classes, using the same 1,000 training and 100 validation sketches per class and five epochs per run. Adding centered x/y coordinates improved the LSTM from 63.27% to 65.20% and the Transformer from 58.23% to 64.11%. More layers helped in this budget: the 3-layer BiLSTM reached 69.84%, and the 6-layer Transformer reached 66.23%. Increasing Transformer width to 256 did not help, while changing heads from 2 to 4 to 8 made only a small difference. These are single-seed, short-budget validation results, so they should not be compared directly with our earlier full-budget or test scores. The inputs still use point tokens; this is a geometry-feature comparison, not the XAI stroke encoder. Code, configs, metrics and conclusions are on my branch.
